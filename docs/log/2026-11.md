## 2026-11-02: a second Linux, one installer family at a time (R40, V14; roadmap item 23)

**What the owner decided.** A friend asked to try the conversion with
Bazzite. The owner: the product is to become distro-agnostic (Ubuntu,
Bazzite and others), and Fedora is functionally finished. Its owed rows
(the one click run with no hand, R33, R39) stay owed in `STATUS.md`.

**What was read.** Bazzite's Secure Boot needs its own key enrolled by a
person; it installs through Anaconda's `ostreecontainer` or `bootc`
command, has fetched its image from the internet mid-install, and keeps
`/usr` read-only. Ubuntu's desktop installer reads `autoinstall.yaml`.
Sources: `docs/research/future-paths.md`, "Other Linux distributions".

**What the code says.** `data/releases.json` already carries `Family`
and `Installer`. Fedora is still written into the stick's `grub.cfg` and
the handoff's `EFI/fedora` paths, `make-kit.sh`, the installer's
`post.rs` and settle-in's `bootentry.rs`; the old non-goal said nothing
outside `upgrade_/linux/` knew what was installed, which was not so.

**What it changes in the record.** `architecture.md` gains "Linux releases
beyond Fedora: one seam per installer"; the "Fedora only" non-goal
becomes "one release per stick, no picker, until a second family holds".
New risk R40, critical (an installer that stops after the last click
leaves no system). V14 is passed per family. Roadmap item 23; the owner's
choice of the second family is owed.
